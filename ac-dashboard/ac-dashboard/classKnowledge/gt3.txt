CAR CLASS: GT3
Profile: ~1300 kg, 500–600 bhp, slick tyres, ABS + traction control, moderate-high downforce.

BRAKING:
- ABS-equipped: brake hard and late, but ABS kicking in early just scrubs speed — aim for threshold pressure just below ABS activation.
- Trail-brake into medium/slow corners to rotate the car; GT3s push wide on entry if you release the brake too early.

THROTTLE:
- TC lets you get to power early, but flooring it mid-corner triggers TC and bogs the exit — feed throttle progressively from the apex.
- Short-shift out of slow corners to keep the car settled and avoid TC cuts.

BALANCE / TYRES:
- Heavy car: manage front tyre temps; smooth inputs and trail braking prevent front graining and understeer.
- Understeer-biased by design — rotate with the brakes and patience, not more steering lock.

COMMON MISTAKES: braking too early (ABS safety net unused); too much steering angle causing understeer; early full throttle triggering TC.
COACHING FOCUS: late threshold braking, trail-brake rotation, progressive throttle out of slow corners.
