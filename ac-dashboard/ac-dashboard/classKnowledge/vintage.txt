CAR CLASS: Vintage / Classic
Profile: light, modest power, narrow period/bias-ply tyres, little or no downforce, no driver aids, weak (often drum or early disc) brakes.

BRAKING:
- Weak brakes with little stopping power and a tendency to lock: brake EARLY, gently, and in a straight line. No ABS.
- Only mechanical grip — there's no aero to help slow the car.

THROTTLE:
- Low-grip tyres: throttle must be gentle, especially RWD classics that snap into oversteer.
- Momentum is everything — carry speed THROUGH corners rather than point-and-squirt.

HANDLING:
- Lots of body roll and slow weight transfer; reward smooth, early, deliberate inputs. The car rewards flow, not aggression.
- Slides look fun but scrub speed — tidy is fast.

COMMON MISTAKES: braking too late/hard and locking up; aggressive throttle causing snap oversteer; throwing the car at corners instead of flowing.
COACHING FOCUS: early gentle braking, momentum and lines over braking points, smooth throttle to avoid snap oversteer.
