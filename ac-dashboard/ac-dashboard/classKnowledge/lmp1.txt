CAR CLASS: LMP1 (Le Mans Prototype)
Profile: ~900 kg, 900+ bhp hybrid, enormous downforce, closed cockpit, slicks, carbon brakes.

BRAKING:
- Even more downforce than F1 in fast sections. Carbon brakes — late, hard, then modulate pressure as speed and downforce fall.
- Hybrid energy recovery under braking shifts brake feel/balance — stay smooth.

THROTTLE:
- Big aero grip and often 4WD hybrid deployment: traction is strong, so you can deploy early in medium/fast corners.
- Slow corners are still traction-limited — progressive throttle.

DOWNFORCE:
- Fast corners are astonishingly quick; commitment is everything. An unnecessary lift in a high-speed corner loses huge time.

COMMON MISTAKES: not trusting the aero in fast corners; braking too early; abrupt inputs upsetting the hybrid/brake balance.
COACHING FOCUS: commit in high-speed aero corners, late modulated braking, smoothness to keep the hybrid/brake balance stable.
