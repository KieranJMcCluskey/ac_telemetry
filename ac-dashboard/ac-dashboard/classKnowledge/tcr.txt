CAR CLASS: TCR (Front-Wheel-Drive Touring)
Profile: ~1200 kg, ~340 bhp, FWD, sequential gearbox, modest downforce, road-derived tyres.

BRAKING:
- Front weight bias + FWD: heavy braking loads the front. Trail braking aids rotation, but too much locks the inside front.
- Left-foot braking is common to settle the car and reduce lift-off understeer.

THROTTLE:
- FWD means power understeer: early or aggressive throttle washes the front wide on exit. Be patient — straighten the car, THEN accelerate.
- A tighter line and using kerbs keeps exits straight for early-but-clean throttle.

HANDLING:
- Lift-off oversteer rotates the car on entry — use it deliberately, but it bites if abrupt.
- Brake-induced rotation beats steering input for getting the nose in.

COMMON MISTAKES: power understeer from early throttle; too much entry speed washing the front out; over-slowing then being unable to rotate.
COACHING FOCUS: trail/left-foot braking to rotate, patient throttle to beat understeer, straighten before power.
