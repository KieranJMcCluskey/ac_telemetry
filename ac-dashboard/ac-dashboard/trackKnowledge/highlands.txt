- A conventional mix of medium and slow corners with a couple of real braking zones and a flowing middle.

LAYOUT — FULL (~12.2 km):
- Begins with the technical loop, then breaks out onto a long, fast, flowing run through the landscape — long straights, fast sweepers, a tunnel and a dam-wall section, with significant elevation change.
- The fast sweepers reward commitment and precise placement; a small line error scrubs a lot of speed over their length.
- Several long straights — the lap time is in the EXIT of the corner that precedes each straight.

KEY RISKS: the fast flowing sections punish a poor line over their full length; long straights mean a weak preceding-corner exit is expensive; watch braking points into the slow corners that follow long fast runs (very high entry speed).
COACHING PRIORITY: nail the exits of the slow/medium corners that feed straights; commit and place the car well through the fast sweepers; brake confidently but early enough into the slow corners after the fast sections. Use the speed-trace and braking-zone data as the primary reference here.
